# ADR-0002: Player skill matters more than raw power

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Decision owners:** JAC Works team

## Context
All three developer interviews independently prioritize player skill, encounter knowledge, execution, and decision-making over simply having larger character numbers.

## Decision
JAC Works will be skill-driven. Gear, progression, and builds may matter and may provide power, but they must not replace the need to play well.

A skilled player should be able to outperform expectations through execution, encounter knowledge, positioning, timing, resource management, and build understanding. Content must not reduce primarily to raw-stat checks.

This ADR does **not** decide the exact amount of vertical power progression.

## Alternatives considered
### Gear/power-first progression
Rejected as a core direction because all three interviews place substantial value on mastery and personal execution.

## Consequences
- Combat and encounters need meaningful skill expression.
- Balance must preserve room for mastery.
- Progression systems should complement rather than automate success.
- Exact power curves remain a separate unresolved decision.
