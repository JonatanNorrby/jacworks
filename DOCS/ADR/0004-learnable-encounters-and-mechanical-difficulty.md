# ADR-0004: Learnable encounters and mechanical difficulty

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Decision owners:** JAC Works team

## Context
All three interviews value encounters that players learn through attempts and mastery. All three prefer higher difficulty to evolve mechanics and execution requirements rather than merely inflate enemy health and damage.

## Decision
JAC Works encounters should have a learnable mechanical foundation while retaining enough variation or pressure to require active play.

Higher difficulties should primarily become harder through mechanisms such as:
- new mechanics;
- overlapping or combined mechanics;
- tighter timing/execution;
- reduced margins for error;
- increased tempo;
- more demanding but still understandable enemy behaviour.

Raw HP/damage increases may support tuning but must not be the primary definition of difficulty.

Failure/retry should support learning, and players should be able to retry failed encounters without unnecessary downtime.

This ADR does **not** decide how often a mechanic failure should cause an immediate wipe versus a recoverable disadvantage.

## Consequences
- Encounter mechanics need readable rules and feedback.
- Difficulty variants should be designed mechanically, not only numerically.
- Rapid iteration/retry is part of the encounter loop.
- Wipe lethality remains an unresolved design decision.
