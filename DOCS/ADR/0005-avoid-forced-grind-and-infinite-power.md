# ADR-0005: Avoid forced grind and infinite power progression

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Decision owners:** JAC Works team

## Context
All three interviews reject or strongly resist progression that depends on excessive repetition or endless raw stat growth. They differ on how much vertical power progression should exist, but agree it should not become an endless treadmill.

## Decision
JAC Works will not rely on forced repetitive grinding or infinite raw-power progression as its long-term retention model.

Power progression, if present, must have a practical endpoint/cap. Long-term play should be able to continue through mastery, harder content, alternative builds/classes, achievements, cosmetics, challenges, new encounters, or other non-infinite-power goals.

Repeated content can be rewarding, but progression should not require excessive repetition of already-mastered content.

This ADR does **not** define the exact power curve, drop rates, deterministic reward systems, or number of repetitions considered acceptable.

## Consequences
- No endless Paragon-like raw-power treadmill as the core progression.
- Reward design should respect player time.
- Replayability must come substantially from gameplay/mastery/content rather than mandatory stat grind.
- Exact vertical progression remains unresolved.
