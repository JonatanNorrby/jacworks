# ADR-0003: Builds and gear should change gameplay

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Decision owners:** JAC Works team

## Context
All three interviews prefer talents, gear, and build choices that alter abilities, rotations, synergies, tools, trade-offs, or playstyles over systems dominated by small numerical bonuses.

## Decision
Meaningful build choices in JAC Works should primarily change **how the player plays**.

Good build effects can change ability behaviour, rotations, resource interactions, utility, synergies, encounter tools, or playstyle. Pure numerical bonuses may exist, but should not be the primary identity of the build system.

Classes should support distinct gameplay identities rather than feeling like cosmetic variants of the same combat pattern.

This ADR does **not** decide class count, role structure, respec rules, or how builds are unlocked.

## Consequences
- Talent/item design should be evaluated for gameplay impact, not only DPS value.
- Build diversity requires mechanics that support different approaches.
- Avoid unnecessary talent/item-system bloat.
- Respec and progression details remain separate decisions.
